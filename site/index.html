What to look at

  • Whether the page reads well mid-run, not just when studied.
  • Typography and palette: both are measured from the source workbooks rather than chosen, so the site should feel related to the sheets.
  • Anything that looks wrong in the data — identities, EGO gift tiers and keywords, fusion recipes, floor packs.

Art is reproduced from the game and the community wiki, and only the images this one page uses are published here.
