Floor 1 Sacs

Explanation: The new ego gift "Spiderweb Entangled in Red" that we are gift searching from the start allows for killed sinners to stay dead even between floors. This makes it so that to buff Ryoshu you only need to kill the HoS IDs once in floor 1 and Ryoshu will be buffed for the rest of the run. You can do this by fielding the IDs together with Ryoshu and killing them through Ryoshu's evade skill. After killing these IDs, you can go back to using the team above as you normally would use a team.
Warning: Since the ego gift makes it so that all allies stay dead if they die once, if anyone from the main team above dies you need to remember to revive them in the shop.

Skill Replacement Warning (Important)

This section is added as a warning, specifically about skill replacement. In MD, you can replace skill 1s and skill 2s with skill 3s through the shop, which is usually beneficial. However, for some IDs, replacing a skill completely (replacing all skill 1s with skill 3s for example) will deactivate some conditional fusion gifts, because they depend on the skills and not the IDs. This page lists all IDs in this team that can be affected by this.

Pequod Heathcliff

  • Skill 3 doesn't gain poise, thus it doesn't count as a poise skill (it will still get the coin power though).
  • Keep at least 1 Skill 2 to not lose the ego gift effects.

EGO Gifts

Starting Gifts

Gift Search

For this team, the most important EGO gifts are the tier 4 fusion ego gifts of Bleed and Poise, alongside the important BL gifts and fusion gifts from Yield My Flesh to Claim Their Bones and Mnestic Experience packs.
Additionally, if you aren't sure about generally useful ego gifts, you can check this page.

You could also start with the poise ego gifts, but I found the bleed ego gifts to be more useful at the start.

Floors to Pick

Floor 1

Automated Factory

+
  • Don't forget to kill all HoS sacs in this floor.
  • Get Haunted Shoes in this floor and fuse it with Frozen Cries for Hoarfrost Footprint.
  • For the next floors, you can focus on poise fusions first.
Floor 2

Hell's Chicken

  • Tango and Needle are always useful, though not required.
  • Contaminated Needle can allow you to immediately have 99 bleed potency on the enemy which is quite useful. This ego gift is also not required if you don't get it, but useful.
  • If you get the chicken event you can use the burn chicken to stack burn with the burn IDs in the team. The rupture chicken can be used as a fusion fodder.
Floors 3-4

The Noon of Violet

  • Filial Love gives +20% damage based on the bleed count of the enemy.
  • Leaked Enkephalin is much less of a priority, but it helps with keeping a sinking stack so that you can benefit from sinking related debuff ego gifts without having sinking IDs.
Floor 3

Full-Stopped by a Bullet

  • Even though HoS Ryoshu doesn't have a pride pierce skill 3 to benefit more from Darkflame Smoking Pipe, the extra attack power up is still quite nice to have.
  • Gear Sharpnel is always useful since it gives some boost to critical damage alongside defense level up and defense power up to all IDs that have poise.
  • CQC Manual is always useful if there are any IDs with ammo in the team.
Floors 5 to 106 packs to choose from

Line 1

  • False Halo gives +2% damage modifier per hit, up to +6% damage per skill, up to a total of +108% damage if a fight lasts that long. Since the cap of +6% is per skill and not per turn, it scales faster with IDs that have additional attacks.
  • Snake Slough gift is less important than False Halo but still useful. It gives 4 defense level up to all allies, and it also gives defense level up buff / 2 offense level up buff (up to 10). So this gift on its own gives 4 defense level up and 2 offense level up and it can scale more if you have other gifts that give defense level up.

Mnestic Experience

++
  • The Book of the Homeland Swordplay is an important fusion gift for up to +55% slash damage and +2 offense level up on slash skills. For BL IDs, it gives +1 coin power, +15% crit damage and makes S Corp. Hong Lu count as a Blade Lineage ID.
  • Crushed Wooden Manacle inflicts 1 defense level down next turn on hit using a slash skill (2 times per skill) while also giving up to +12% damage to all allies based on speed difference, which doubles and becomes +24% damage if the enemy has bind.
  • Ohra Bond inflicts 1 bind next turn on the enemy on crit (2 times per turn), gives 1 poise on hit against enemies that have bind (2 times per turn), inflicts up to 5 offense level down on enemies based on bind if all enemies have bleed, and if all enemies have bind all allies gain 3 poise.
  • Baekja Water Dropper gives 2 damage up and 2 protection to allies that have assist defence, while also giving 2 haste and up to +30% damage based on speed difference to the #1 slotted ID.
  • Flagroot Cane Sword gives 1 slash damage up next turn if an ally wasn't hit by an enemy, and if they were hit by an enemy it gives 4 poise and 1 crit damage up this turn and next turn.

Yield My Flesh to Claim Their Bones BokGak

+
  • Red Tassel is very important for Mnestic Experience fusion so make sure to get it.
  • Cultivation fusion gives +2 slash power up and +35% damage to all allies.
  • Ragged Bamboo Hat gives +50% slash damage to all allies and makes BL IDs always crit, and Old Dopo Robe gives additional +50% slash damage to all allies and buffs BL Meursault.
  • Rusted Hilt gives +1 slash power up to all allies and Hardwood Liquor Cup gives 1 protection and some SP if you lose a clash, these two are less of a priority.

LCB Regular Check-up Bokgak

+

  • 4 beneficial ego gifts for burn, with some damage and clashing buffs for the sinners and debuffs for the enemies.
  • Modified Extraction: AEDD is an ego gift that can benefit all IDs and give them some offense level.

Line 2

  • Bridle is a useful ego gift since it can give up to 50% more HP to all of your IDs when upgraded.
  • Metronome is only mildly useful and not as important to get compared to Bridle.
  • Since Bridle is a keywordless tier 4 ego gift it can be up to luck to get it in the shop or through clear rewards. While missing this ego gift is not an issue, it is definitely better to get it than not.
